[B2] Fan Zhang, Valentin Bazarevsky, Andrey Vakunov, Andrei Tkachenka, George Sung, Chuo-Ling Chang, Matthias Grundmann · "MediaPipe Hands: On-device Real-time
     Hand Tracking" · CVPR 2020 Workshop on Computer Vision for AR/VR · arXiv:2006.10214
     Where   : hand recovery layer (modules/hand_recovery.py, holistic.worker.js "method C"), Error-Analysis-v2 §extractor
     Topic   : two stages — palm detector (BlazeFace-like, run only when tracking is lost) + 21-point hand landmark model on a crop from the previous frame
     How     : explains our two observed failure modes: (1) tracking follows the previous frame's box, so at a LOW FRAME RATE a fast hand leaves the box and is lost;
               (2) the palm detector runs on a small full-frame image, so small / far / side-on hands are missed. Our recovery layer re-runs the landmark model on a
               zoomed, forearm-aligned crop from the pose wrist = the paper's own two-stage idea, applied where Holistic lost the hand.
     Status  : USED + ADAPTED · Verified: Y

[B3] Valentin Bazarevsky, Ivan Grishchenko, Karthik Raveendran, Tyler Zhu, Fan Zhang, Matthias Grundmann · "BlazePose: On-device Real-time Body Pose tracking" ·
     arXiv:2006.10204 · 2020
     Where   : the pose part of Holistic (33 body points); our hand-ROI centre/size comes from the pose wrist / elbow / finger points
     Topic   : detector + tracker; 33 keypoints; >30 fps on a phone

[B4] Ivan Grishchenko et al. · "BlazePose GHUM Holistic: Real-time 3D Human Landmarks and Pose Estimation" · arXiv:2206.11678 · 2022

[B5] Kyunggeun Roh, Huije Lee, Eui Jun Hwang, Sukmin Cho, Jong C. Park · "Preprocessing Mediapipe Keypoints with Keypoint Reconstruction and Anchors for Isolated
     Sign Language Recognition" · LREC-COLING 2024, 11th Workshop on the Representation and Processing of Sign Languages, pp. 323–334 · ACL Anthology 2024.signlang-1.36

C. SIGNING-ACTIVITY DETECTION, SEGMENTATION, ONLINE RECOGNITION   (phase 3)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
[C1] Amit Moryossef, Ioannis Tsochantaridis, Roee Aharoni, Sarah Ebling, Srini Narayanan · "Real-Time Sign Language Detection using Human Pose Estimation" ·
     ECCV 2020 Workshops (SLRTP), LNCS 12536, pp. 237–248 · doi:10.1007/978-3-030-66096-3_17 · arXiv:2008.04637
     Where   : the "is this signing?" gate before any word is spotted (Error-Analysis-v2 issue 1–2; signing gate in this phase)
     Topic   : frame-level signing / not-signing from pose optical flow; linear 80 %, LSTM 91 % on DGS Corpus, <4 ms, browser demo
     How     : the closest prior work to our gate: pose MOTION alone separates signing from not-signing well on a corpus, BUT its features are body-wide flow — our
               ghost words come exactly from body motion without visible hands, so our gate additionally requires hand evidence (C5).
     Status  : CONTEXT / ADAPTED · Verified: Y

[C2] Abhilash Pal, Stephan Huber, Cyrine Chaabani, Alessandro Manzotti, Oscar Koller · "On the Importance of Signer Overlap for Sign Language Detection" · arXiv:2303.10782 · 2023
     Where   : Error-Analysis-v2 (evaluation protocol), why our gate is tuned on recorded live requests and checked on held-out people/days
     Topic   : sign detection accuracy drops 4–6 % (relative) once train/test signers do not overlap
     How     : argues against tuning on the same signer → our live data is split by recording day, and the test signer of TSL51 stays unseen
     Status  : CONTEXT · Verified: Y

[C3] Amit Moryossef, Zifan Jiang, Mathias Müller, Sarah Ebling, Yoav Goldberg · "Linguistically Motivated Sign Language Segmentation" · Findings of EMNLP 2023,
     pp. 12703–12724 · arXiv:2310.13960
     Where   : our tagger (modules/sequence.py / segment.py: per-frame rest / sign / transition probabilities) and the spotting decoder
     Topic   : BIO tagging of signs and phrases from pose; optical flow helps shallow models; 3D hand normalisation hurt (noisy 3D pose)
     How     : our tagger is the same family (frame tagging → segments); their finding that noisy hand estimates hurt matches our hand-loss analysis
     Status  : CONTEXT · Verified: Y

[C4] Low Jian He, Harry Walsh, Ozge Mercanoglu Sincan, Richard Bowden · "Hands-On: Segmenting Individual Signs from Continuous Sequences" · IEEE FG 2025 · arXiv:2504.08593
     Where   : Error-Analysis-v2 (hand-shape features for boundaries), future work
     Topic   : BIO-tagging transformer using HaMeR hand features + 3D skeleton angles; SOTA on DGS Corpus, BSL Corpus
     How     : evidence that HAND features (not body motion) carry the sign / non-sign boundary — the principle of our hand-evidence gate
     Status  : CONTEXT · Verified: Y

[C5] Ronglai Zuo, Fangyun Wei, Brian Mak · "Towards Online Continuous Sign Language Recognition and Translation" · EMNLP 2024, pp. 11050–11067 · arXiv:2401.05336
     Where   : modules/stream.py (StreamingTranslator: sliding window, lookahead confirmation, duplicate merging), Error-Analysis-v2 §stream vs batch
     Topic   : online CSLR with an isolated-sign model + sliding window; augmented signs for co-articulation; post-processing removes duplicates of overlapping windows
     How     : our stream is the same design (isolated-sign bank + sliding window + confirm/merge). Their observation that a window trained on full videos but
               applied to short clips degrades matches our "stream words ≠ batch words in 66 % of utterances" (Error-Analysis-v1).
     Status  : CONTEXT / COMPARED · Verified: Y

[C6] Takao Obi, Wang Yusong, Koji Inoue, Kotaro Funakoshi · "Toward Signing Activity Projection in Sign Language Interaction" · arXiv:2606.09424 · 2026
     Where   : end-of-utterance decision (hands-down wait, web LIVE_END_OPTIONS) — future work
     Topic   : Voice Activity Projection adapted to signing; predicts HOLD vs SHIFT from hand / eye / mouth pose cues (DGS Corpus); hand cues most informative
     How     : a learned "is the turn over?" predictor could replace our fixed hands-down wait; cited as motivation for an adaptive end
     Status  : CONTEXT · Verified: Y (author list from an aggregator: [verify])

[C8] Zhizhong Li, Derek Hoiem · "Learning without Forgetting" · ECCV 2016, LNCS pp. 614–629 · doi:10.1007/978-3-319-46493-0_37 · arXiv:1606.09282
     (journal version: IEEE TPAMI [verify year/volume])
     Where   : models/model-v10/onnx/gate_tagger.onnx (phase-3 signing gate), Error-Analysis-v2 §2
     Topic   : add a new capability to a trained network while keeping its old outputs, via distillation to the old model's soft outputs
     How     : the gate = the v10 frame tagger fine-tuned with a NEW kind of non-sign (hands up but not signing); on real signing it is distilled to the v10
               tagger (KL to its soft outputs) so the old behaviour is kept; at inference it may only VETO long non-sign runs (≥ 1.2 s)
     Status  : ADAPTED · Verified: Y (ECCV version)

[C7] Kapitanov, Kvanchiani, Nagaev, Kraynov, Makhliarchuk · "HaGRID — HAnd Gesture Recognition Image Dataset" · WACV 2024, pp. 4572–4581 · arXiv:2206.08219
     Where   : F4; Error-Analysis-v2 (idea of an explicit "no gesture" class to reduce false triggers)
     Topic   : 554,800 FullHD images, 18 gestures + a "no_gesture" class, 37,583 subjects, 0.5–4 m from the camera
     How     : the "no_gesture" negative class is the same idea as our explicit non-sign / idle evidence; not used for training (static images, not motion)
     Status  : CONTEXT · Verified: Y
